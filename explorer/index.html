600 telephone calls written by LLMs, side by side. Every conversation exists in twelve versions: four ways of generating it × three prompts, all on the topic of a real recorded call.

Each block is one turn; width = words, shade = speaker. Dots are listener turns: just “uh-huh”, “yeah”, “right”. Click a block to jump to that turn.

Generated with Vicuna-13B v1.5 and Mistral-7B-Instruct (4-bit). Turns are shown exactly as analysed: scripted “Hello!” openings and non-speech residue removed. The human calls come from the Switchboard corpus, whose transcripts are licensed by the LDC and are not shown; only their turn lengths are used here.
